### What makes this person a strong candidate for the AI 100?

Rahul Gupta is a strong candidate for the H2O AI 100 because his work combines real-world AI deployment, responsible AI research, patented invention, and academic community building at enterprise scale. He is a Senior Applied Science Manager at Amazon, where he has led Responsible AI science across major AI systems: first founding Responsible AI science for Alexa, then leading Responsible AI for Amazon Nova, and now serving as the overall Responsible AI lead for Amazon's AGI / Frontier AI Assets organization.

Rahul's impact spans the criteria emphasized by the H2O AI 100: impact, innovation, leadership, and trust. He has led teams of scientists and engineers working on AI safety, red-teaming, fairness, privacy, evaluation, mitigation, and launch-readiness for large-scale conversational AI and foundation models. His work has helped translate responsible AI from abstract principle into practical mechanisms for evaluating and improving systems used by large populations.

His research impact includes 200+ papers in top venues, 5K+ citations, and influential contributions to trustworthy AI and NLP. His work includes BOLD, a widely used dataset and metric suite for measuring bias in open-ended language generation; FLIRT, a feedback-loop approach to in-context red teaming; privacy-preserving and fairness evaluation work for language systems; and frontier-model evaluation work such as a threshold exceedance framework for CBRN uplift evaluation. He has also contributed to rigorous evaluation methods for agentic AI systems.

Rahul is also an inventor of 25+ patented or patent-pending technologies. His Alexa-era patents helped support fairer, more private, and more equitable NLP experiences through mechanisms for cohort determination, model configuration, user-data processing, and language-understanding evaluation. His Nova-era patent filings on generative language models and synthetic-data generation support responsible AI methods that improve the safety profile of frontier foundation models.

Beyond his own technical contributions, Rahul has invested heavily in academic community building. He has helped organize TrustNLP, Trustworthy Speech Processing, and the SemEval Unlearning Challenge, creating shared research infrastructure for bias, privacy, robustness, unlearning, and trustworthy language technologies. These workshops and challenges have helped shape research agendas, create publication opportunities, and give academic teams shared benchmarks and venues for recognition. He has also delivered invited keynotes, talks, and panels at venues including IEEE SLT, COLM, and IJCAI-ECAI workshops, helping connect industrial responsible-AI practice with the broader research community.

Rahul has also built bridges between industry and academia through large-scale research programs. He led the Nova Trusted AI Challenge, which received 100+ university applications and supported selected academic teams through multi-million-dollar awards for frontier-model safety research. He has served as an Amazon scientific point of contact for Academic Research Award collaborations representing more than $20M in funded academic research.

Rahul represents the kind of AI leader the H2O AI 100 is designed to recognize: a hands-on research leader and enterprise builder whose work advances AI that is more useful, measurable, safe, fair, and trustworthy in the real world.

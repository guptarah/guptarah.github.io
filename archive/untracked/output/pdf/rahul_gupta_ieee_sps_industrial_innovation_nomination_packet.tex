\sectionhead{6. Impact of the Contributions and Evidence Supporting the Claims}
\textbf{Industrial impact and evidence:} Nova models have been adopted by tens of thousands of customers, in part attributable to the strong safety profile built by Rahul's team. Nova has also been endorsed by multiple third-party evaluators as a top-line safety model. Amazon Science's \textit{Independent evaluations demonstrate Nova Premier's safety} (\url{https://tinyurl.com/2y7yqgp3}) reports black-box stress testing and red-team exercises by evaluators such as PRISM Eval and ActiveFence. The Nova Premier critical-risk evaluation (\url{https://tinyurl.com/25hagozh}) further states that Nova Premier was safe for public release under Amazon's Frontier Model Safety Framework. External coverage from ABNewswire (\url{https://tinyurl.com/2252v6bz}) reported that Anthropic and Amazon led in independent quantitative AI safety testing, while Quantum Zeitgeist (\url{https://tinyurl.com/26qau3vy}) summarized Nova Lite safety evaluation work as a robust FMSF assessment with third-party validation.

His patent portfolio provides additional evidence of Responsible AI concepts becoming production mechanisms. For example, during his Alexa leadership period, the technology reflected in \textit{Cohort determination in natural language processing} (\url{https://tinyurl.com/275fb62t}) helped identify underperforming cohorts in NLP systems and use that signal to improve model performance across user groups. The technology was deployed for millions of Alexa customers, contributing to improved experiences for minority and underrepresented customer groups; it is one example from Rahul's technical contributions.

\textbf{Academic impact and evidence:} The academic impact is visible through public venues and programs that create reusable research artifacts and bring universities into frontier-model safety work. Rahul's Google Scholar profile (\url{https://tinyurl.com/27tungke}) reports close to 5,000 citations, reflecting broad research uptake across speech, NLP, and Responsible AI. The TrustNLP workshop series, Trustworthy Speech Processing, and the SemEval Unlearning Challenge provide venues and shared tasks for research on trustworthy language and speech processing. As an example of workshop leadership he has helped build, a 2026 analysis of six years of TrustNLP (\url{https://tinyurl.com/22ua8qn8}) documents the workshop's growth from 8 archived proceedings papers in 2021 to 41 in 2026, with 144 proceedings papers across six editions. The Nova Trusted AI Challenge connected frontier model safety questions to university teams after hundreds of universities applied, and the program has supported 10 universities for safety research year over year. Together, these programs show that Rahul's work has shaped not only deployed systems but also the research ecosystem that studies and evaluates Responsible AI.

\sectionhead{7. Brief Biography or Link to CV}
Rahul Gupta is a Senior Applied Science Manager focused on Responsible AI in Amazon's AGI / Frontier AI Assets organization. His work spans safety, evaluation, red-teaming, fairness, privacy, and mitigation capabilities for frontier foundation models, Amazon Nova, and other initiatives in the Amazon AGI division. He has 25+ patented or patent-pending technologies and publications across NeurIPS, ICLR, ACL, EMNLP, FAccT, ICASSP, and other venues, and helps organize TrustNLP, Trustworthy Speech Processing, and the SemEval Unlearning Challenge.
